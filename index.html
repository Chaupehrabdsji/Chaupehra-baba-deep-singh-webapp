About Us

Our Group

Chaupehra Baba Deep Singh Ji is a Sikh kirtan group based at Gurdwara Nanaksar, Surrey, BC. We come together in the spirit of seva and simran — performing kirtan at Gurdwara Nanaksar and community events across the Lower Mainland.

Gurdwara Nanaksar Surrey

Located at 14212 88th Ave, Surrey, BC (Cloverdale), Gurdwara Nanaksar is the third Nanaksar Gurdwara in Canada — established in 2003 under the blessing of Sant Baba Gurdev Singh Ji. Daily Kirtan Deewan and monthly Pooranmashi programs are held for all sangat.

Baba Deep Singh Ji

Baba Deep Singh Ji (1682–1757) was a revered Sikh scholar, soldier, and martyr. A devout student of Guru Gobind Singh Ji, he became head of the Shahid Misl and devoted his life to the protection of the Sikh faith, Gurbani, and the Harmandir Sahib. He is remembered as a symbol of unwavering courage and dedication.

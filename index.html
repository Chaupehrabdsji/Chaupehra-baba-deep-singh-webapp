Who Was He?

Born in 1682, Baba Deep Singh Ji spent his life as a student of the Guru, a scribe of scripture, and a warrior for the defenseless. At 75, he marched against a 20,000-strong army to reclaim Harmandir Sahib — fulfilling a vow that his head would fall at the Golden Temple. He kept it.

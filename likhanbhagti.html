    Instructions

    1. Use a new notebook to write ਵਾਹਿਗੁਰੂ. Write your name, age, address, parent's name, and phone number on the cover.
    2. Beautiful Gurmukhi handwriting and a clean notebook will receive a special prize.
    3. To be part of the event and receive Guru's blessing, notebooks must be submitted to Gurdwara Nanaksar Sahib before Sept 13, 2026.
    4. Participants from all over North America can arrange notebook submission by contacting us through our Contact page.
    5. All participants will receive a participation gift. Those who submit a completed notebook (full count for their age group) will also receive a prize.
    6. "Waheguru" can be written in any language. There is no restriction on pen or pencil. Gurmukhi script is given preference in prize consideration.
    7. The Award Ceremony is on Sunday, Sept 25, 2026 at 4:30 – 6:00 PM in Surrey, BC.

Instructions

  1. The last date for registration is 30 June 2026.
  2. All participants must show age proof and a photo ID at the time of submitting their notebooks.
  3. The submission centre will be fixed in your state or nearest city. The list of centres will be uploaded by 30 June 2026.
  4. Please write strictly according to the syllabus for your age category (see table above).
  5. Every participant who completes the full syllabus will receive a Consolation Prize.
  6. "Waheguru" can be written in any language. There is no restriction on pen or pencil.
  7. No notebook or diary will be provided — participants must arrange their own.
  8. Only one registration per email ID is valid. Use a different email for additional registrations.
  9. Please ensure the mobile number provided is an active WhatsApp number so updates can be shared.
  10. All updates will be shared through the registration scanner from time to time.
  11. This seva is organised by the sangat and gurmukhs. Local management names and numbers will be available in the scanner link.
  12. Write the page number on every page. On the first page, write: Name, Father's Name, Location, Mobile Number, Age, Address.
  13. The number of prizes may vary by location.
  14. Participants who complete the syllabus will receive a coupon on the day of checking. Checking dates will be announced after 30 June 2026. Major prizes will be given at the main Samagam.
  15. All sangat members are requested to attend the Samagam. Some prizes are reserved for participants present at the Samagam.
